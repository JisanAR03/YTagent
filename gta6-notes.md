## Plan vs. source - needs your attention
- The roadmap PDF (Step 10) says pre-order **"before November 19"**. Rockstar's wording is **"before November 20"** (i.e. through launch day). The PDF is one day more conservative, so it is safe, but the real deadline is later. Time-zone handling on the storefront is not stated - pre-order well before launch day rather than testing it.
- The free GTA+ month is **digital only**. A physical code-in-box copy does not get it.
- Treat the [via-search] rows as "verify on the live Rockstar page before you make a decision or a video claim from them".
